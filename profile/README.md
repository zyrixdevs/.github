### 01 / What we do

**ZYRIX DEV** is a design-led software studio interested in beautiful interfaces and useful systems. Our work and experiments move across web development, developer tools, automation, and creative technology.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zyrixdevs/.github/main/profile/assets/capabilities.svg" alt="Illustrated areas of focus: web experiences; tools and automation; creative engineering." width="100%" />

- **Web experiences** — responsive sites and interfaces with clarity, personality, accessibility, and purposeful motion.
- **Tools & automation** — practical software, bots, and workflow ideas that help people do more with less friction.
- **Creative development** — 3D, motion design, visual systems, and interactive experiments that make the web feel alive.

### 03 / Our toolkit

The portfolio is built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Vite**, and **Three.js**, with **GSAP** and **Lenis** for interaction. We use **Cloudflare Pages** for delivery and **Playwright** for browser checks.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zyrixdevs/.github/main/profile/assets/stack.svg" alt="Technology stack: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Three.js, GSAP, Lenis, Vite, Cloudflare and Playwright." width="100%" />

### 04 / How we think

We believe the strongest work starts with understanding the problem, not selecting an effect. Our guiding sequence is simple:

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zyrixdevs/.github/main/profile/assets/process.svg" alt="Four-part design and development approach: explore, design, build, refine." width="100%" />

**Clarity over noise.** Make things easy to understand. &nbsp; **Craft over shortcuts.** Care about the details. &nbsp; **Utility over novelty.** Build for real use.
